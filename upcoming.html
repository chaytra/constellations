Phase 3: Upcoming Interactive Celestial Features

⏳ In Active Development

Features slated for release in the upcoming milestones:

  • 🌌
    Interactive HTML5 Sky Canvas: A lightweight, mobile-friendly interactive star chart widget calibrated specifically for observers across India (latitudes 8°N to 35°N).
  • 📅
    2026–2027 Indian Stargazing Calendar: Timetable for major meteor showers (Geminids, Perseids), planetary oppositions (Saturn & Jupiter), and lunar eclipses with viewing tips.
  • 🌑
    Live Moon Phase & Dark-Sky Window Calculator: Calculates optimal moonless observation nights for deep-sky stargazing based on local sunset and moonrise times.
  • 📥
    Downloadable Printable A4 Field Cheatsheets (PDF): High-contrast, inverted black-on-white printable star charts for field use with red torches.
  • 🗣️
    Multilingual Stargazing Pronunciation Audio: Authentic Sanskrit and Hindi audio clips pronouncing traditional Nakshatra and star names.
  • 🏕️
    Indian Dark-Sky Reserve Directory: Curated guide to prime astrotourism locations in India, including Hanle (Ladakh), Spiti Valley, Kutch, Pachmarhi, and Coorg.
